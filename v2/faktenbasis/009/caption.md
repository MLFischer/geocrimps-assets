# GeoCrimp #009 — Caption

Chalk is the one piece of gear almost nobody questions. Science does.

Magnesium carbonate counters the moisture on your skin, and most lab studies measure higher finger–hold friction with a light layer of chalk. But there's a limit: a thick coat of loose powder can act like a layer of tiny rolling particles between skin and rock — and friction drops again.

Fun fact: one early lab study famously concluded that chalk *reduces* friction. The debate has kept sports scientists busy ever since.

Source: Li et al. (2001), J. Sports Sciences · Amca et al. (2012), J. Sports Sciences · Clarke et al. (2024), Proc. IMechE Part P.

Light dust or full powder glove — how do you chalk up?
